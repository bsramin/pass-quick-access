# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1, as its code of conduct.

In short: be respectful and considerate. Harassment and exclusionary behaviour
are not welcome, and maintainers may remove contributions or contributors that
do not follow the Covenant.

To report a concern, contact a@ramin.it. Reports will be handled confidentially.

The full text is available at the link above and at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
